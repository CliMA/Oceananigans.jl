## Notes

- Reproduce: `julia --project validation/split_stepping_ffsl/discriminators.jl` (CPU, Julia 1.12).
- 1: the split N = 32 and unsplit (Δt = 96 min) FFSL runs of the divergent ridge gyre diverge identically; the blow-up
  cells lose about 1.6–2 times their volume horizontally per long step next to a dry western neighbour (negative
  thickness after the horizontal step). The ridge case also runs unmodified on `jsw/ffsl-advection` with the same
  numbers (vertical WENO5: overshoot 0.48, uniform deviation 2.84e-12; vertical first-order upwind: no overshoot,
  4.2e-15), so the growth of the uniform-tracer deviation to ~1e-12 is a property of FFSL with vertical WENO5 in
  this divergent setup, not of the long step.
- 2: the passive undershoot at C ≈ 2.4 is identical split and unsplit: the horizontal FFSL scheme (Workstream B) is not
  strictly positivity-preserving at C > 1 in a two-dimensional flow. It accounts for −3e-4 of the NPZD minimum at N = 32;
  the rest (−1.1e-3 at cfl ≤ 0.2, −3e-2 at cfl = 0.5) comes from the explicit part of the vertical step or its coupling
  with the sub-cycled sources and sinking detritus.
- 4: the fold face j = Ny + 1 (for RightCenterFolded its sʸ mirrors the face Ny) carries a swept Courant number
  above 1 at N = 32 on the RightCenterFolded grid (1.23) and at N = 64 on both grids (2.30, 1.93, not truncated there),
  in runs whose uniform tracer and ∫σc stay at round-off (results_tripolar.txt).
