## Notes

- Reproduce: `julia --project validation/split_stepping_ffsl/conservation_and_error.jl tripolar` (CPU, Julia 1.12).
- 20×32×3 TripolarGrid (H = 100 m, halo 7) with GridFittedBottom islands at the poles, z-star, barotropic flow
  u = 2 sin φ cos λ, v = −2 sin λ (m/s) across the fold, free surface bump 0.1 m, Δt = 3 hours, 80 days.
  (The Workstream B setup with H = 4 m and a 1 m bump blows up dynamically at these Δt; H = 100 m and a 0.1 m bump do not.)
- "C swept at fold" is the maximum swept Courant number on the x-faces of rows Ny−1, Ny and the y-faces Ny−1, Ny, Ny+1.
  At N = 32 it is 1.23 (RightCenterFolded) and 1.33 (RightFaceFolded), and at N = 64 it is 2.3–2.7: FFSL slow tracers are
  advected across the fold at C > 1 with uniform deviation ≤ 1.5e-14 and ∫σc drift ≤ 4e-15.
  Components at N = 32 (separate diagnostic): RightCenterFolded max |sʸ| = 1.03 (face Ny−1), 1.23 (face Ny);
  RightFaceFolded max |sʸ| = 1.32 (Ny−1), 1.15 (Ny), 1.00 (the fold face Ny+1), max |sˣ| = 1.00 (row Ny−1).
- WENO5 slow tracers fail at N = 32 (C proxy 2.8–3.2); FFSL is stable up to N = 64 (truncated at Cmax + 1 = 4).
