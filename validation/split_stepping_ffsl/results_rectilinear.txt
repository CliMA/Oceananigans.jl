## Notes

- Reproduce: `julia --project validation/split_stepping_ffsl/conservation_and_error.jl [gyre|ridge|baroclinic]` (CPU, Julia 1.12).
- The dynamics Δt (3 minutes) is fixed and identical for every N and slow scheme; slow tracers are passive. Both slow
  schemes use adaptive implicit (cfl 0.5) vertical WENO5; the fast tracer `fast` is FFSL with explicit vertical WENO5.
- "C swept" is the volume-based Courant number the FFSL step uses (maximum over faces and long steps). It saturates at
  4.00 = maximum_courant_number + 1, where the swept region is truncated.
- D is the maximum net horizontal outflow of a cell during a long step relative to its volume. The FFSL horizontal step
  is operator-split from the vertical one, so D > 1 leaves a negative thickness after the horizontal step. This is the
  stability limit of FFSL in the "ridge" and "baroclinic" cases (FFSL fails at N = 32 with D ≈ 2.4 and 9.9), where the
  flow is forced into the immersed ridge and the net outflow of the cells next to it (a vertical Courant number) exceeds 1.
  An unsplit FFSL run with Δt = 96 minutes (the N = 32 long step) fails in the same way and in the same cells, (11,1,2)
  and (12,1,1), whose thickness after the horizontal step would be −0.97 and −0.61 of the initial one (checked for the
  ridge gyre), so this is a property of the FFSL scheme of Workstream B, not of the long step.
- The "gyre" case (streamfunction flow around the ridge, D ≈ 0) is the clean comparison: WENO5 fails at N = 32
  (C ≈ 2.7) while FFSL stays stable up to N = 64, where the swept region is truncated (C proxy 5.4 > Cmax + 1).
- FFSL errors vs the WENO5 N = 1 reference are dominated by the scheme difference (FFSL N = 1 vs WENO5 N = 1:
  L² 3.5e-2) and barely grow with N; "L² vs same N=1" isolates the error of the long step.
- WENO5 "stable at N = 16, C proxy 4.0" in the baroclinic case refers to a local, transient maximum; in the sustained
  flows the WENO5 limit is N = 16 (C ≈ 1.35–1.7).
- Uniform-tracer deviation and ∫σc drift are ≤ 2.4e-15 in every stable run of the gyre and baroclinic cases.
  In the divergent ridge gyre, the slow FFSL uniform tracer deviates by up to 2.7e-12 (N = 1, 2) and the fast FFSL
  uniform tracer by up to 2.9e-11: this setup is at the edge of stability of FFSL with vertical WENO5 even unsplit
  (the smooth tracer overshoots by 0.55 at N = 1), and the same deviation (2.8e-12) is obtained with the unmodified
  `jsw/ffsl-advection` branch without any splitting; with a first-order upwind vertical scheme it is 4.2e-15.
