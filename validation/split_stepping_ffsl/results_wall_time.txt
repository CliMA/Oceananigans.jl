## Notes

- Reproduce: `julia --project validation/split_stepping_ffsl/wall_time.jl 16 64` (CPU, 1 thread, Julia 1.12); the first long step
  (compilation) is excluded and 20 long steps are timed. Indicative only; the machine is shared.
- N = 16 (WENO5) and N = 64 (FFSL) are the maximum stable N of the 16×8×6 gyre table. On the 64×32×24 grid Δt and the
  gyre speed are scaled to keep the Courant numbers, but the stable N was not re-verified there.
- The FFSL long step is cheaper than WENO5 per slow step here, but most of the gain from N = 16 to N = 64 is already
  captured: with the slow group taking 1/N of its unsplit cost, the fast dynamics and fast tracers dominate.
